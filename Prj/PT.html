Project: Perfect Transportation – End-to-End Transportation & Supply Chain Management

1. Challenge
The organization needed end-to-end visibility and control over the product value chain, from raw-material planning and manufacturing through shipment, transportation, delivery, installation, and final start-up at the customer's site. Information was distributed across Oracle databases, ERP systems, GPS platforms, and external applications, making it difficult to maintain a consolidated view of product location, delivery status, transportation costs, and carrier performance. The solution also needed to support domestic and international shipments, provide timely alerts, and enable controlled information sharing with customers while respecting enterprise security restrictions.
2. Objectives
- Integrate production, logistics, transportation, GPS, and customer information into a centralized monitoring environment. - Track each product throughout its lifecycle, from raw-material availability through delivery, installation, and start-up. - Enable transportation planning before manufacturing completion and monitor planned versus actual milestones. - Measure delivery performance, carrier effectiveness, transportation costs, and quality outcomes. - Support operational reporting for domestic and international shipments. - Implement role-based access controls and alerts for operational events and exceptions. - Provide secure customer-facing capabilities for accessing and submitting shipment-related information and images, including from mobile devices.
3. Actions
- Integrated information from Oracle databases, ERP systems, GPS platforms, and external applications to provide a consolidated view of product status and location. - Developed transportation-planning and shipment-tracking capabilities covering planned and actual production dates, shipment schedules, carriers, delivery dates, and current product location. - Implemented daily product tracking across the value chain, including raw-material warehousing, manufacturing, shipment, transportation, delivery, installation, oil filling, and start-up. - Incorporated GPS information to monitor products in transit and improve visibility into transportation progress. - Developed operational and management indicators covering on-time delivery, on-time pickup, carrier performance, transportation costs and cost breakdowns, cost per unit, quality claims, and quality results. - Supported reporting for both domestic and international deliveries, accommodating their different operational requirements. - Integrated the application with Oracle Portal to manage permissions for creating, modifying, viewing, and deleting information. - Implemented an alert mechanism to generate operational notifications and exceptions requiring attention. - Developed a separate Internet-facing component using Microsoft IIS, ASP, HTML, and JavaScript to support customer-facing functionality without requiring the restricted Oracle Application Server environment to access external Internet sites directly. - Enabled customer-related information and images to be captured through the Internet and reviewed from mobile devices. - Maintained detailed shipment records covering customer, destination, transportation unit, dimensions, weight, carrier, planned and actual dates, and delivery status.
4. Results
- End-to-end product traceability: Established visibility across the product lifecycle, from raw-material planning to installation and start-up at the customer's site. - Improved transportation planning: Enabled logistics teams to plan shipments in advance and monitor progress against planned production and delivery milestones. - Consolidated operational visibility: Integrated ERP, Oracle, GPS, and transportation information into a common monitoring environment. - Performance measurement: Provided indicators for delivery reliability, pickup punctuality, carrier performance, transportation costs, and quality outcomes. - Exception management: Introduced alerts to identify operational events and conditions requiring attention. - Controlled information sharing: Established differentiated access controls for internal users and customer-facing functionality. - Extended customer visibility: Enabled Internet-based access to relevant information and images, including through mobile devices. - Domestic and international support: Provided shipment records and reporting capabilities for different transportation scenarios and delivery requirements.
5. Business Impact
Transformed transportation management from disconnected production, shipment, and logistics activities into an integrated, traceable supply-chain process. By combining ERP, Oracle, GPS, carrier, and customer information, Perfect Transportation established a common operational view of each product's journey and provided the information needed to monitor delivery commitments, control transportation costs, evaluate carrier performance, and support customer service.
