Project: Revenue Analytics & Global Data Platform

Click any section header below to instantly expand or collapse details.

1. Challenge
Commercial information was distributed across multiple ERP systems operating in different countries, with inconsistencies in data structures, definitions, formats, and business processes. These differences made it difficult to establish a reliable, consolidated view of global revenue performance, particularly for pricing, promotions, sales, and distribution. The organization needed a centralized analytical platform capable of integrating heterogeneous data sources, improving data quality, and providing actionable commercial insights while accommodating different regional privacy and regulatory requirements. The project also operated within a global implementation program with demanding delivery expectations, budget constraints, and a need to demonstrate business value quickly.
2. Objectives
- Establish a cloud-based Data Warehouse/Data Lake as a centralized foundation for global commercial information. - Integrate data from multiple international ERP systems into a consistent analytical environment. - Improve data quality through cleansing, standardization, reconciliation, and harmonization. - Provide dashboards and drill-down capabilities to analyze sales, pricing, promotions, distribution, key customers, revenue, and competitive performance. - Adapt data granularity and analytical capabilities to regional privacy and regulatory requirements. - Deliver the implementation using an Agile approach, maximizing scope and business value within the available timeline and budget.
3. Actions
- Analyzed business requirements and designed the integration of geographically distributed ERP systems with a cloud-based Data Warehouse/Data Lake. - Coordinated data integration and modeling activities to consolidate commercial information from multiple countries into a common analytical framework. - Addressed data quality challenges through cleansing, standardization, reconciliation, and harmonization of commercial data, particularly pricing and promotional information. - Developed dashboards, graphical indicators, and analytical capabilities that enabled users to move from high-level performance metrics to detailed investigation of business drivers. - Supported analysis across sales, pricing, promotions, strategic distribution, key customers, revenue performance, and competitive information. - Designed regional variations in data granularity and analytical access to accommodate differing privacy and regulatory requirements, including more restrictive detail-level access in European environments and broader permitted drill-down capabilities in Mexico. - Applied Agile delivery practices to prioritize functionality, coordinate implementation activities, and deliver the planned scope incrementally. - Focused on maximizing implementation scope and functionality within tight delivery and budget constraints, demonstrating the value of the solution early in the program.
4. Results
- Successful full-scope implementation: Mexico became the only country among approximately eight initial implementations to complete the entire project scope. - Accelerated delivery: Delivered the complete implementation in less than one year. - Early delivery of full functionality: Completed the full scope of Stage 1 within the first four months, exceeding the functionality delivered by other participating countries. - Efficient use of budget: Delivered the Stage 1 scope using a smaller portion of the budget than other implementations, demonstrating strong delivery efficiency. - Consolidated commercial intelligence: Established a common cloud-based analytical foundation integrating information from multiple ERP systems. - Improved analytical capabilities: Enabled business users to analyze commercial performance across sales, pricing, promotions, distribution, customers, and revenue. - Regional compliance support: Accommodated different levels of data detail and analytical access according to applicable regional requirements. - Extended engagement: Demonstrated business value and delivery performance led to an extension of the engagement, enabling further development of the platform and its capabilities.
5. Business Impact
Established a global commercial analytics platform that transformed fragmented, geographically distributed ERP information into a consolidated foundation for revenue intelligence and decision-making. The solution strengthened visibility into commercial performance and supported analysis of pricing, promotions, distribution, customer management, and revenue strategy while accommodating regional data privacy requirements. The successful Mexican implementation also demonstrated that the full solution could be delivered rapidly and efficiently, establishing a strong reference for the global program and contributing to the continuation of the engagement.
